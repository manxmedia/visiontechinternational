Who we are?

Vision Tech International is a future-focused ICT and telecommunications engineering company based in Centurion, Pretoria, South Africa.

Since our beginnings in 2015 as Fibre Direct, our business has grown from fibre network deployment to a diversified provider of telecom infrastructure, IoT solutions, software engineering, and smart digital technologies.

We combine African market experience with global technology standards to deliver reliable, scalable, and intelligent solutions for enterprises, homes, municipalities, and regional telecom operators.

Our rebrand reflects our broadened capabilities and our commitment to innovation, sustainability, and next-generation infrastructure development.

Vision

To be the leading architect of a future where technology and energy infrastructure unlock human potential across Africa and beyond.

Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable, and sustainable ICT and telecommunication solutions that transform operations, empower communities, and enhance customer experience.
